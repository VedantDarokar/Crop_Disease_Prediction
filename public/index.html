Recommended Application Hours 6:30 AM – 10:30 AM • 4:30 PM – 7:00 PM

Avoid midday application when temperatures exceed 30°C to eliminate droplet vaporization and leaf stomata closure.

Rainfast Uptake Threshold 4 to 6 Hours Prior to Rain

Systemic fungicides (e.g., Azoxystrobin, Tebuconazole) require at least 4 hours of rain-free foliage for full translaminar absorption.

Applying sprays to leaves dripping with heavy morning dew causes immediate runoff and pesticide dilution.
